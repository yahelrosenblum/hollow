# Hollow in the classroom

Hollow is a hands-on way to show how sound behaves. It needs a computer with desktop Chrome or Edge, and headphones or speakers. Open https://yahelrosenblum.github.io/hollow/ , press **Clap to begin**, and each student can work on their own.

It simulates a 2D room, so use it to build intuition about waves, not to predict a real building.

## Five activities

Each takes 5 to 10 minutes.

### 1. Why do rooms echo?
Open **Cathedral** and clap. Watch the wave spread, hit the columns and the walls, and come back.
- *Ask:* Where does the wave go first? Why does the sound last longer than the clap?
- *Idea:* Sound is a wave of pressure that reflects off hard surfaces. A big hard room keeps the energy for a long time.

### 2. What do soft surfaces do?
In **Build**, draw a box of foam, then clap on one side and compare. Or use **Measure** to see the echo time before and after.
- *Ask:* Did the echo get shorter? Did it change for high sounds and low sounds differently?
- *Idea:* Absorbers take energy out of the wave. Foam absorbs high frequencies more than low ones.

### 3. Why can you hear a bass through a wall but not the words?
Open **Next door** and shut the door in **Build**. Listen from the other room.
- *Ask:* Which sounds get through, and which don't?
- *Idea:* A heavy thin panel blocks high frequencies better than low ones. Frequency matters.

### 4. Can two sounds make silence?
In **Sources**, press **Two opposed**. Listen, then drag yourself to one side.
- *Ask:* Why is it nearly silent in the middle? What happens when you move?
- *Idea:* Waves add. Where a peak meets a trough, they cancel. This is the idea behind noise-cancelling headphones.

### 5. Can sound go backward?
Open **Time mirror**, clap, wait, then open **Time** and press **Rewind time**.
- *Ask:* Why does it only work in this room? What happens if you draw a wall while it rewinds?
- *Idea:* The wave equation doesn't care which way time runs, but loss does. Without loss, a wave can retrace its path.

## Questions for discussion

- How would the cathedral sound different if the walls were wood?
- What would you build to make a room with no echo?
- Why does it matter that this simulation is 2D?

## Tips

- Students need headphones in a shared room.
- If the page says WebGPU isn't available, try desktop Chrome or Edge, or update the browser.
- Rooms save automatically in the browser, and can be shared as a link.
